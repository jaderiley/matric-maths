# Afrikaans glossary for Matric Maths

Target: the Afrikaans used in the DBE's own NSC Wiskunde papers and memoranda
(Vraestel 1 / Vraestel 2). When a term here conflicts with what a real DBE
Afrikaans paper uses, the DBE paper wins: fix this file and tell the coordinator.

## Hard rules
- Change words, never maths. Everything inside `$...$` stays byte-identical,
  EXCEPT the words inside `\text{...}` (e.g. `\text{ or }` -> `\text{ of }`,
  `\text{ and }` -> `\text{ en }`). Keep the spaces inside `\text{ }` as they are.
- Numbers outside `$` stay exactly the same (same digits, same `{,}` decimal comma).
- Do not touch `id`, `archetype`, `svg`, `topic`, `url`, `memo`, `updated`, `topics`.
- Capitalised exam words stay capitalised: TWO -> TWEE, ONE -> EEN, THREE -> DRIE,
  HENCE -> GEVOLGLIK, NOT -> NIE.
- Natural, correct Afrikaans in the register of an exam paper. Verb-second /
  verb-final word order, double negation (`... nie ... nie`), `'n` for "a".
- Currency stays `R`. Units stay (cm, m, km, kg, %). "per annum" -> "per jaar" (p.j.).

## Settled by the DBE Afrikaans exam guidelines (2021)
- Calculus is **Differensiaalrekening** everywhere (titles, subtitles, notes, formula sheet).
- NSC / DBE / CAPS -> **NSS / DBO / KABV**.
- Question references use **Vr.** (Vr. 1.1.2); **V1 / V2** means Vraestel 1 / 2 only.
- Afrikaans day/month abbreviations take a full stop: Vr. 23 Okt.
